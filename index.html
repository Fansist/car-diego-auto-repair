About the Shop

Frequently Asked Questions

A few honest answers before you call or stop by.

Do you work on my make of car?
Yes. We service American, European, and Asian vehicles, domestic and foreign, all makes are welcome at our Adams Avenue shop.
Can you do my STAR smog check here?
Yes, we're a certified STAR smog check station, which means we can perform your official California smog check on site.
My car failed smog. Can you fix it and re-test?
Yes. We diagnose and repair failed-emissions issues right here, so you don't have to shop the fix out to a separate repair shop before re-testing.
